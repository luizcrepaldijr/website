AgriTech:

Smart irrigation platform with IoT

Transforming soil data into strategic irrigation decisions.

AgriTech is a smart agriculture platform developed to monitor, analyze, and optimize irrigation in medium and large-sized farms.

The project was initially implemented on an avocado-producing farm, focusing on increasing water efficiency, reducing waste, and ensuring greater operational predictability.

The challenge

Lack of continuous soil moisture monitoring and decisions based on estimates, impacting productivity and water consumption.

Problems

  • Lack of real-time data
  • Decisions based on estimates
  • Water waste
  • Low operational predictability

Needs

  • Continuous monitoring
  • Automatic recommendation
  • Precise irrigation control
  • Data-driven management

The solution

We developed an integrated IoT platform applied to agriculture, consisting of:

1. Field Infrastructure

Layer responsible for continuous collection and secure transmission of data in an agricultural environment.

  • Soil moisture monitoring sensors
  • Gateway with LoRaWAN technology
  • Solenoid valve controller
  • Flow meter
  • Starlink integration for remote connectivity
  • Remote monitoring and control system

The sensors collect continuous data on:

  • VWC (Volumetric Water Content – Soil Moisture)
  • Soil temperature
  • Electrical conductivity (EC)

This data is transmitted via LoRaWAN to the central platform.

2. Smart Monitoring Platform

The AgriTech platform allows the owner to track, in real-time:

  • Daily sensor history
  • Comparative charts by monitored area
  • Recommended ideal irrigation ranges
  • Mild or severe deficit alerts
  • Automatic irrigation recommendation (in gallons per tree/day)
  • Estimated system activation time

Additionally, the system features:

  • Centralized dashboard
  • Farm map with georeferenced points
  • Detailed table exportable to Excel
  • Sensor and alarm management
  • 24/7 operation with remote support

The system provides full operational visibility and continuous decision support.

3. Intelligence Applied to Irrigation

The platform doesn't just display data; it generates automatic irrigation recommendations based on the ideal parameters defined for each crop. Example analysis:

  • Identification of severe moisture deficit
  • Automatic calculation of water requirement
  • Adjustment recommendation (e.g., 3.4 gallons/tree/day)
  • Estimated irrigation time

This enables fast and informed decisions, reducing risks and increasing efficiency.

Results

AgriTech's implementation was not limited to installing sensors or dashboards. The project structured a new operational base, allowing for informed technical decisions, greater predictability, and waste reduction across the entire production chain.

Impacts were felt both in the field and in the strategic management of the operation:

Real-time monitoring of soil quality

Reduction in water waste

Technical standardization of water management

Full operational visibility

Scalability for multiple areas and crops

Structured base for precision agriculture

Strategic Differential

AgriTech's edge isn't just in data collection, but in the integrated architecture between field, connectivity, and applied intelligence.

The solution combines:

  • Long-range IoT (LoRaWAN)
  • Remote connectivity
  • Applied data analysis
  • Intuitive interface
  • Operational scalability

It's not just a monitoring tool: AgriTech is a smart irrigation management platform, driven by data.
