AgriTech: Smart IoT platform for irrigation based on real-time soil data

Challenge

Lack of continuous soil moisture monitoring and decisions based on estimates, impacting productivity and water consumption.

Solution

IoT infrastructure with soil sensors integrated via LoRaWAN into a platform with a centralized dashboard, automatic alerts, and technical irrigation recommendations.

Challenge

Decentralized records in spreadsheets and lack of structured criticality classification, compromising traceability and prioritization.

Solution
