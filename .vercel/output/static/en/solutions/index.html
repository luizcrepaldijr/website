Digital transformation

We empower your business through intelligent, connected, and data-driven solutions.

We help organizations unlock the full potential of their data and technologies by integrating intelligent tools, automating processes, and enabling real-time insights that drive strategic decision-making.

We help consumer goods companies drive their digital transformation focusing on real impact. With practical experience in industrial operations, we define projects, technologies, and strategies that generate concrete results, through scalable and business-aligned roadmaps.

We develop AI-based solutions designed to solve specific operational challenges. By combining our industry expertise with advanced AI capabilities, we create solutions that increase efficiency, boost productivity, and generate measurable gains.

We structure pipelines, analytical models, and visualization layers that transform raw data into actionable insights for operation and decision-making.

Technical and strategic training for teams, ensuring autonomy, adoption of implemented solutions, and sustainability of the digital transformation.

Industry 4.0

We connect the physical and digital worlds to increase visibility, control, and industrial performance.

We operate in industrial environments where the lack of integration between machines, systems, and data limits operational efficiency. We implement Industry 4.0 solutions that connect the factory floor to the digital environment, allowing more predictable, safe, and data-driven operations.

Design and implementation of robust architectures for industrial data collection and integration, connecting operation and management systems securely.

Protection of networks, data, and critical assets, reducing operational risks and ensuring production continuity in industrial environments.

Development and programming adjustments necessary for integration and proper functioning of industrial systems.

Continuous technical support to ensure operational stability, rapid failure response, and maintenance of implemented systems.

Engineering

We transform complex ideas into viable and efficient technical solutions.

We offer end-to-end engineering services for projects that require technical rigor, planning, and precise execution. We act from conception to implementation, connecting engineering, technology, and operation to reduce risks and optimize results.

Technical management with focus on scope, time, cost, and quality, ensuring alignment between teams and efficient execution.

Initial technical analyses to guide strategic decisions and evaluate project feasibility in an objective and applied way.

Preparation of cost estimates and necessary documentation to support investment and execution decisions.

Advanced modeling and simulations to test scenarios, anticipate risks, and optimize solutions before physical implementation.

Complete and integrated projects, aligned with operation needs and the technologies involved.

Agriculture 4.0

We help producers and agricultural operations make better decisions using real field data.

We apply technology to transform information from soil and environment into practical intelligence, allowing better irrigation, waste reduction, productivity increase, and more sustainable and predictable operation.

Continuous collection of data such as moisture at different depths, temperature, and electrical conductivity, offering a precise view of actual crop conditions.

Support for irrigation decisions at the right time and exact amount, reducing waste and generating significant savings in water use.

Real-time visualization, measurement history, alerts, and tracking of hydraulic and productive performance of the operation.

Analysis of water use with conversion into financial indicators, facilitating cost control and operation planning.

Integration with satellite connectivity solutions, ensuring continuous monitoring even in regions without local infrastructure.

IoT (Internet of Things)

We connect assets, processes, and environments to generate operational intelligence, predictability, and efficiency in real time.

We help companies see in real time what happens in their operation, applying IoT to transform physical signals into practical operational intelligence.

We make it possible to anticipate failures, optimize resources, reduce waste, and make faster, safer, and data-driven decisions.
