IoT for agriculture

Water efficiency, productivity, and smarter decisions in the field

Agribusiness faces the challenge of producing more with fewer resources and greater sustainability.

IoT provides farmers to understand the soil in real time and make precise decisions, reducing waste and increasing productivity.

What we monitor

Strategically distributed sensors capture:

Soil moisture at different depths
Soil and ambient temperature
Electrical conductivity (fertility and salinity)
Conditions that directly impact irrigation

What we deliver

This data feeds a platform that delivers:

Real-time metrics
Actionable irrigation recommendations
Automatic calculation of applied water
Cost conversion to USD
Alerts for urgent soil conditions

We provide on-site and remote support
