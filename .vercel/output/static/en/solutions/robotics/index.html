Robotics: industrial automation at a new level

From concept to integration of robotic systems in industrial environments to increase productivity, safety, and operational efficiency.

We believe that robotics goes far
beyond automation.

Industrial robotics is the new layer of intelligence that connects machines, people, and data to increase productivity, safety, and operational predictability.

With applied robotics, it is possible to:

Integrate robots into production systems and logic
Enable flexible automation to optimize critical industrial processes
Perform autonomous inspection in high-risk areas and continuous operation
Continuously measure productivity and operational performance
Technologies

Tailor-made robotics solutions for each industrial environment

Humanoid robots

Applied in scenarios that require mobility and interaction with human environments, preparing factories for the flexible automation of the future.

Safe interaction with people and systems
Multi-purpose and reconfigurable task execution
Support for logistics and maintenance activities
Advanced mobility across different terrains
Preparing the plant for next-generation automation

Quadruped robots

Quadruped robots for autonomous inspection and monitoring. Advanced mobility and sensing for complex industrial environments, such as:

Autonomous inspection of machines and high-risk areas
Detection of air and gas leaks
Monitor PPE usage by employees
Measurement of temperature, noise, and vibration
Autonomous security patrols
Continuous field data collection

Collaborative robots (cobots)

Cobots are mainly applied in processes that require repeatability, precision, and reduced physical effort, such as:

Automated palletizing and depalletizing
Component assembly and sub-assembly
Parts handling and transfer
Packaging and end-of-line operations
Direct support for human operators
Process standardization and speed gain

Total integration with the factory

Each robot operates as an active part of the production flow, fully synchronized with existing industrial plant systems.

  • Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES)
  • Automation and supervision (SCADA, PLCs)
  • Communication with industrial networks and IoT
  • Integration with quality and maintenance systems
  • Operation synchronized with the factory’s processes and control systems

Robotics connected to data

Real-time operational data for better decisions. Robotics generates continuous visibility into performance, quality, and efficiency.

  • Productivity and cycle time monitoring
  • Logging downtime, failures and anomalies
  • In-process quality control
  • Collection of operational and environmental conditions
  • Integration with analytics platforms

Predictive maintenance and continuous improvement

From automation to operational intelligence, we transform robots into strategic sensors to continuously evolve the operation.

  • Reduction of unexpected failures
  • Increased asset availability
  • Optimized resource usage
  • Continuous process improvement
